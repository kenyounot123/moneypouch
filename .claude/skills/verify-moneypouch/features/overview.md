# Overview

## Sub-features

- The sidebar's `Add` button with its `⌘K` chip, and `Add` in the top bar below `md`. Either, or ⌘K or Ctrl+K, opens the composer in `dialog#palette` over a scrim. See [composer.md](composer.md), Q6.
- Spent total, category breakdown, and chart for one period, read by `Spending` over `Period` (`app/models/spending.rb`, `app/models/period.rb`). `/?period=week|month|year` picks it, and anything else means month. The heading reads `Spent this week`, `Spent in October`, or `Spent in 2026`. Under it, the day strip and `Day N of M`: days through today are slate (`bg-data`), the rest the pale track. Gold (`bg-highlight`) on the Overview is only for what needs the user: the `From your bank` count and the dots on rows that need a category. Category bars are slate: the first is `bg-data-strong`, every other one, `Uncategorized` included, `bg-data`.
- Every category is spending or income (`categories.income`), and every user starts with a built-in `Income` category (`User#income_category`). Spending is `Transaction.spending`: counted (`Transaction.counted`: not a transfer and not excluded), and either in a spending category (any sign) or money out with no category. Money in a spending category nets against it (a `+$20 #Groceries` refund lowers Groceries and the total by $20). Money in an income category, such as a typed `paycheck +2400`, stays out.
- Where it went lists each category whose period nets above zero, largest first, with shares of the listed categories' sum. A category that only got money back is left out but still lowers the total. It lists rows without a category as `Uncategorized`, and the period's dates on the right, such as `Oct 1–31`. With no spending it reads `Nothing spent this week.`, `Nothing spent this month.`, or `Nothing spent in 2026.`
- The chart slot sits under the category rows. Its header (`_chart_header.html.erb`, `[data-field=readout]`) shows a readout on the left and `nav[aria-label=Chart]` (`Spending | Summary`, the current one `aria-current=true`) on the right. The readout is a heading `[data-chart-target=heading]` and an amount `[data-chart-target=spent]` in `text-readout`. In the Summary chart it is a heading, two figures `[data-chart-target=income]` and `[data-chart-target=spent]`, each with its bar color, and a `Net` line `[data-chart-target=net]` that keeps its height and hides when the net is not positive. At rest it names the column marked `data-active` (today's column, or the current or focused period). `chart_controller.js` rewrites it from the hovered column's `data-chart-{heading,spent,income,net}-param` on `mouseenter` (and `focus` for links), moves `data-active` there, and restores the resting text on `mouseleave`/`blur`. Under the bars a dollar axis (`_axis.html.erb`) draws a gridline per tick with a short label on the right (`$0`, `$5k`, `$2.5k`, `$1.2M`); `WelcomeHelper#axis_ticks` steps from zero past the peak by 1, 2, 2.5, or 5 times a power of ten, at most five steps, and the last tick is the top of the 140px plot. The URL carries `chart=spending|summary` (anything else means spending, and `Chart#to_param` leaves spending out of links) and, with `summary`, `focus=YYYY-MM-DD` (any date inside the period; a future or unreadable date clamps to the current period). Every link keeps the others: Week/Month/Year keep `chart` and drop `focus`, the chart toggle keeps `period` and drops `focus`.
- Spending chart: one column per day (week, month) or month (year). Every bar is `bg-data-soft` except the `data-active` column's, which is `bg-data-strong`. A column through today is a `div[data-chart-target=column]` that reads `Spent today`, `Spent on Oct 3`, or `Spent in October` with its amount (`$0.00` on a day with nothing spent). Future columns are empty with no hover. Labels sit under the columns: every column, centered, when the period has 12 or fewer, else every 7th from the first (`Oct 1`, `Oct 8`, ...).
- Summary chart (`chart=summary`): the current period and the five before it, following the period toggle (six weeks starting Monday labelled `Sep 7`, six months labelled `Sep`, six years labelled `2025`), from `Summary` over `Period#previous` (`app/models/summary.rb`, `app/models/period.rb`). Each is a link to `focus=<first day>` with a green income bar (`bg-income`) and a slate spent bar (`bg-data`), then the period label. The current period's bars are `opacity-50` and it reads `present` under the label. The readout's two figures name the bar colors, so there is no legend. It reads `September` (`Oct 5–11`, `2025`, `November (present)`), `Income $7,890.00`, `Spent $3,977.12` (whole dollars on the year toggle, so large figures stay on one line), and `Net +$3,913` in `text-success` only when the net is positive. The figures sit on their own row under the heading and toggle, and the net line keeps its height, so the chart does not move on hover. A period that spent more than came in has no net line, no red, and no minus, the same as Trends. `trends_tone_test.rb` guards both pages. The link's `aria-label` reads `Sep · income $7,890 · spent $3,977`. Income is `Transaction.incoming`: counted, and either in an income category or money in with no category.
- Focus (`chart=summary&focus=…`): the chart stays, the readout rests on the focused period, the focused column is `aria-current=true` with the level-3 background, and the other columns' bars dim to `opacity-40`. Clicking the focused column links back to the unfocused summary. Below the chart, `_focus.html.erb` shows the period name (`September`, `Oct 5–11`, `2025`), then an `Income` flow (income grouped by name) when income is positive and a `Spent` flow (the period's categories) when spending is nonzero or has categories. Each flow is its total, a stacked bar scaled against the larger flow (no bar when the total is not positive, e.g. refunds outweigh spending), and a legend of the top 2 plus `N more`. An empty period reads `Nothing spent.`. There is no `Nothing came in.` and no `← All months`. A period older than the six still opens from the URL, with every column dimmed.
- The Week, Month, and Year links in `nav[aria-label=Period]` swap `turbo-frame#spending`, which holds everything under the composer, and push `/?period=…` to history. The current one carries `aria-current=true`. The composer's text and focus stay. Back and Forward restore the period.
- Recent: the 5 latest kept transactions. Money in reads `+$…` in `text-success`; transfers and excluded rows stay tertiary. The heading row has `Show all` on the right (to `/transactions`, `data-turbo-frame=_top`), preceded by `Checking your banks` with a spinner while a sync runs. A paired transfer's category reads `Transfer to <full account name>`, the one place a bank account shows in Recent. The label truncates to one line, so a long name never reaches the row's name or amount. To see it, pair two bank rows in different accounts with long names. With no kept transactions it reads `No transactions yet.` and lists the three ways in, By hand, From a file, and From your bank (`welcome/_empty_recent`, see [import.md](import.md)).
- Excluded: a row marked Excluded (`transactions.excluded`) stays listed but leaves Spending, Summary, income, and net everywhere. `_transaction` rows show an `Excluded` tag after the name and dim the name and amount like a transfer.
- Notes: every `transactions/_transaction` row (Recent, the bank block, the review, `/transactions`) shows its `transactions.note` on its own line under the name, a pencil and the text in `text-secondary`, truncated. A row with no note shows an icon-only `button[aria-label="Add a note"]` chip after the name only while the row is hovered or holds focus. Clicking the note or the chip swaps in `input[aria-label=Note]` (placeholder `Add a note`) styled like the focused category field (`note_controller.js`, one per row). Enter or blur saves through `PATCH /transactions/:id/note` (`transaction[note]`; blank clears it, since `Transaction` normalizes a blank note to nil); an unchanged value just closes. Escape closes and refocuses the note or chip. `mp.mjs hover '<row> p.font-mono'` first, because the chip is `display: none` until the row is hovered.
- Adding or undoing from the composer redirects back to the same `/?period=…`, and the morph refresh updates the total, bars, and Recent in place.
- Every other open Overview of the same user follows within a moment. `Transaction` broadcasts a Turbo `refresh` to `[user, :transactions]` on each create, update, and discard, and the page subscribes with `turbo_stream_from`. Each page morphs at its own URL, so a page on Year stays on Year. The tab that made the change ignores the broadcast because its `request-id` matches.
- Sidebar with navigation. Its bottom is a Settings link to `/settings`, see [theme.md](theme.md). The current page's link carries `aria-current=page`.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Sign in. `/` is the Overview.

## Driving it with mp.mjs

- `mp.mjs key Meta+k --port N` opens the palette. Then `mp.mjs type '[aria-label="Add"]' hello --port N` prints `"hello"`, the field value.
- `mp.mjs key Enter --port N` presses Enter in the focused field.
- `mp.mjs shot <path>.png --port N`, then read the image.
- `mp.mjs resize 375 812 --port N` prints `scrollWidth=375` when the page has no horizontal scroll.
- `mp.mjs query 'Transaction.count'` reads the stored count.
- Chart readout: `goto '/?chart=summary'`, then `text '[data-field=readout]'` reads `<this month> (present)`, `Income`, `Spent`, their amounts, and `Net +…` when it is positive. `hover 'a[aria-label^="Sep"]'` switches it to September; `hover aside` puts it back. On `goto '/?period=year'`, `hover '[data-chart-target=column]:nth-child(9)'` reads `Spent in September` and its amount.
- Gold check: `js '[...document.querySelectorAll("turbo-frame#spending .bg-highlight")].map(e => e.closest("li")?.id || e.parentElement.parentElement.textContent.trim().split("\n")[0])'` lists only `transaction_N` rows that need a category (their dots). The day strip and the chart's today column never appear.

## Gotchas

- The dev server does not rebuild Tailwind. After adding a class, run `bin/rails tailwindcss:build` before a `shot`, or the new utility is missing.
- A refund lowers spending only when it sits in a spending category. Money in with no category counts as income.
- The period starts from `Date.current` in the browser's zone (the `time_zone` cookie). `time_zone_controller.js` writes the cookie after the page loads, so the server reads a new zone one request late. After `mp.mjs zone`, run `goto` twice before you read `Day N of M`. Check a number with `query` and the same `Date`.
- A future-dated row, such as rent typed for the 31st, counts toward the total and draws its bar.
- `bin/rails "dev:transactions[N]"` dates rows from the server's `Date.current` (UTC), so in a zone behind UTC the newest rows can land on tomorrow.
- Driving the live refresh needs two pages on one server, because the development cable adapter is in-process. Boot a second instance, then send its page to the first server with `mp.mjs js 'location.href = "http://localhost:<first port>/"' --port <second>`. Cookies ignore the port, so it stays signed in. `turbo-cable-stream-source[connected]` shows the subscription is live. `insert_all`, as in `dev:transactions`, skips callbacks and broadcasts nothing.
- Handles: `turbo-frame#spending`, the chart toggle `nav[aria-label=Chart] a`, a summary column `a[href*="focus=2026-09-01"]`, the readout `[data-field=readout]` with `[data-chart-target=heading]` and `[data-chart-target=amount]`, a spending column `[data-chart-target=column]`, an axis label `[data-field=tick]`, the total `turbo-frame#spending p.text-display`, the toggle `nav[aria-label=Period] a[href="/?period=week"]`.
- A link inside `turbo-frame#spending` that leaves the Overview needs `data-turbo-frame="_top"`, or Turbo looks for the frame on the next page and shows "Content missing".
- Typing in the composer field renders the preview through `GET /voucher`. Wait on the frame text before a `shot`.
