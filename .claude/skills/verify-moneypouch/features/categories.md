# Categories

## Sub-features

- Both `/categories` and `/categories/:id` add up one span of months (`Category::Span`): `?months=1|3|12` ending with this month, or `?month=YYYY-MM` for one earlier month. A missing or other length is the last 3 months, and `?month=` for this month equals `?months=1`. `nav[aria-label=Period]` offers `October`, `Last 3 months`, `Last 12 months`, the open one `[aria-current=true]`; an earlier month leaves none current.
- Words are the app's: sections `Spent` and `Income` (a category is one or the other), the detail stat `Spent in the last 3 months`, `Income in October`, `Spent in August` (`Spent in July 2025` in another year). A total is counted rows only (not a transfer, not excluded), and a refund comes off a spending category's total. Net is never used here, since elsewhere it means income − spent.
- List: one grid (`category-columns`) holds the column header, each section heading, and each `li#category_<id>`: name, `By month` (12 bars, Nov to Oct, the span's months in `bg-data-strong`), `Of total` (a bar for its part of the section total), the span total (`[data-field=total]`), and the `Manage <name>` button. Section totals (`[data-field=section-total]`) sit in the total column. Below `md` only name, total, and the button show. Rows with something in the span come first by total, then the rest alphabetically, dimmed, with `—`. When a category was last used shows only on its own page. A section with nothing in the span totals `—`. Rows carry no transaction count.
- `Manage <name>` opens a native `[popover]` anchored under the button (`anchored-menu`, flips above near the page bottom): `Rename` field and `Save` (`PATCH`, back to the page it came from), `Merge into` select (submits on change, confirms with the all-time counts), and `Delete` (confirms). `Income` has no menu.
- Detail: `‹ Categories` keeps the span. Stats: the span total (`—` when nothing is listed), `After $X in refunds` for a spending category with money in, `Monthly average` over the last 12 months, `Last transaction`. `nav[aria-label="Spent by month"]` (or `Income by month`) has 12 bar links, each `aria-label="August 2026: $9.00"`, opening `?month=`. The day-grouped list is the Transactions ledger's (see [transactions.md](transactions.md)), lazily paged with the span kept. An empty span reads `No transactions in the last 3 months. The last one was on Jul 7.` with `Show July 2026` when that is outside the span.

## Drive

- `mp.mjs goto '/categories?months=12' --port N`, then `mp.mjs click "button[aria-label='Manage Food']" --port N` and `mp.mjs shot` to see the menu; `mp.mjs key Escape` closes it.
- `mp.mjs click "nav[aria-label=Period] a:first-child" --port N` on a detail page, then `mp.mjs wait '[data-field=empty]'` for a category with nothing this month.
