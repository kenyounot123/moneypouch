# Categories

## Sub-features

- Both `/categories` and `/categories/:id` add up one span of months (`Category::Span`): `?months=1|3|12` ending with this month, or `?month=YYYY-MM` for one earlier month. A missing or other length is the last 3 months, and `?month=` for this month equals `?months=1`. `nav[aria-label=Period]` offers this month's name (`November`), `Last 3 months`, `Last 12 months`, the open one `[aria-current=true]`; an earlier month leaves none current.
- Words are the app's: sections `Spent` and `Income` (a category is one or the other), the detail stat `Spent in the last 3 months`, `Income in October`, `Spent in August` (`Spent in July 2025` in another year). A total is counted rows only (not a transfer, not excluded), and a refund comes off a spending category's total. Net is never used here, since elsewhere it means income − spent.
- List: one grid (`category-columns`) holds the column header, each section heading, and each `li#category_<id>`: name, `By month` (12 bars ending with this month, the span's months in `bg-data-strong` when they have spending), `Of total` (a bar for its part of the section total and the percentage, `[data-field=share]`, such as `27%` or `<1%`), the span total (`[data-field=total]`), and the `Manage <name>` button. Section totals (`[data-field=section-total]`) sit in the total column. Below `@2xl` of `main`'s width (672px, so 1024px wide with the sidebar open and no Help drawer) only name, total, and the button show. Rows with something in the span come first by total, then the rest alphabetically, dimmed, with `—`. When a category was last used shows only on its own page. A section with nothing in the span totals `—`. Rows carry no transaction count.
- Excluding a category: `Exclude from totals` in the Manage menu (`POST /categories/:id/exclusion`, `Categories::ExclusionsController`; `Include in totals` is the `DELETE`) sets `categories.excluded`. Every transaction in it, whatever its date, and every one added, imported, or synced later, leaves the Overview, Trends, Transactions, and day totals, and shows the `Excluded` tag. Including it again restores them, and a row excluded by hand stays excluded. Uncategorized rows always count. The built-in `Income` has no menu, so it can't be excluded. The category keeps its real total and months on `/categories` and its own page, with an `Excluded` tag beside its name, and stays out of the section total and `Of total`. On `/categories` its name, the 12 month bars, and its total are dimmed; on its own page the total and the month chart are dimmed. Its Merge and Delete confirmations add that the transactions count in totals again (when merged into a category that is not excluded). In another category's Merge menu an excluded target reads `<name> (excluded)`, and the confirmation adds that the transactions leave your totals if the picked category is excluded.
- `Manage <name>` opens a native `[popover]` anchored under the button (`anchored-menu`, flips above near the page bottom): `Rename` field and `Save` (`PATCH`, back to the page it came from), `Merge into` select (submits on change, confirms with the all-time counts), and `Delete` (confirms). The built-in `Income` category has no menu and carries a `Default` tag; an income category you made has one.
- Detail: `‹ Categories` keeps the span. Stats: the span total (`—` when nothing is listed), `After $X in refunds` for a spending category with money in, `Monthly average` over the last 12 months, `Last transaction`. `nav[aria-label="Spent by month"]` (or `Income by month`) has 12 bar links, each `aria-label="August 2026: $9.00"`, opening `?month=`. The day-grouped list is the Transactions ledger's (see [transactions.md](transactions.md)), lazily paged with the span kept. An empty span reads `No transactions in the last 3 months. The last one was on Jul 7.` with `Show July 2026` when that is outside the span.
- Bar sizes and the Manage anchor are not `style=` attributes, because the CSP has no `unsafe-inline` for styles. `style_declarations` (`ApplicationHelper`) puts the values in `data-style-declarations-value` and `style_controller.js` sets them through the CSSOM on connect and again on each `turbo:morph-element`, because a morph strips the attribute. Every page with a bar, chart, or axis uses it, so `document.querySelectorAll('[style]')` is empty before the controllers connect.

## Drive

- `mp.mjs goto '/categories?months=12' --port N`, then `mp.mjs click "button[aria-label='Manage Food']" --port N` and `mp.mjs shot` to see the menu; `mp.mjs key Escape` closes it.
- `mp.mjs click "nav[aria-label=Period] a:first-child" --port N` on a detail page, then `mp.mjs wait '[data-field=empty]'` for a category with nothing this month.
