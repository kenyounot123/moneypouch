# Bank sync

## Sub-features

- The Bank sync section on `/settings`, inside `turbo-frame#bank_sync`, between Appearance and Your data. Its state follows `Simplefin::Access#status`: none or `disconnected` shows `Sync transactions straight from your bank.`, a line that SimpleFIN links the bank read-only and costs $1.50 a month, and `Connect your bank`, `pending` shows the account picker, `active` shows the synced accounts, `revoked` shows Reconnect. `Simplefin::Access#lapsed?` reads the latest answered `Simplefin::Sync` (finished with no failure, `lapsed`, or `revoked`, so an `unavailable` or `abandoned` sync does not hide it) and adds the subscription notice.
- Connect: `GET /simplefin/access/new` in the frame. Step 1 lists the four SimpleFIN steps in an `ol`. Pasting a SimpleFIN setup token into `#simplefin_access_setup_token` only fills the field. The `Connect` button (or Enter) submits the form, and while the claim request runs the button reads `Connecting…` and is disabled (Turbo's `data-turbo-submits-with`).
- Pick: checkboxes `simplefin_access[account_ids][]` for `Simplefin::Access#pickable_accounts`, which leaves out an account that replaces a missing one (it waits in Settings for `Same account`, and Start syncing neither syncs nor skips it), the Start from radios `simplefin_access[history]` (`today` or `90_days`), and `Start syncing ↵` (`button.bg-action`), which sends `PATCH /simplefin/access` and queues `Simplefin::SyncJob`.
- Steady state: `Synced N min ago` with `Sync now` behind a hand-drawn sync icon (`POST /simplefin/sync`, which lands on the Overview; while it runs the button reads `Syncing…` and the icon spins), one row per synced `BankAccount` with its bank transaction count, New rows with Skip and Sync it (`PATCH /bank_accounts/:id`; Sync it starts the account 90 days back and queues a sync), `Bring in 90 days` on a synced row whose `starts_on` is later than 90 days back while the access is active (`POST /bank_accounts/:id/history`, the same 90-day start and sync), and Disconnect (`DELETE /simplefin/access`). An account with a SimpleFIN error shows SimpleFIN's own message and Fix on SimpleFIN.
- Missing accounts: a successful sync that leaves out an account sets its `missing_since`, and a synced one is then missing (`BankAccount#missing?`), and the next sync that sends it clears it. Its row reads `SimpleFIN stopped sending this account.` with `Stop syncing` (`PATCH /bank_accounts/:id` with `skipped`) and `Fix on SimpleFIN`. When it is the only missing account with its name and bank, and exactly one account with that name and bank first appeared after the last report that had the missing one (`reported_at`, `BankAccount#replacement`), the row adds `It now sends another account with this name. Is it the same one?` and `Same account` (`POST /bank_accounts/:id/replacement`) takes the place of `Fix on SimpleFIN`. That one replacement's New row stays hidden until then, and a synced replacement offers no `Bring in 90 days`. `Same account` moves the replacement's rows and SimpleFIN id onto the old account, deletes the replacement, and queues a sync. Each relink first tests whether the bank kept its transaction ids. When any old row shares an id with the replacement's rows (`BankAccount#take_over`) or, for rows still in doubt (`bank_transactions.id_in_doubt`, `BankTransaction.with_id_in_doubt`), with the next report (`adopt_new_ids`), the bank kept them: rows pair by id only and every other row is a separate purchase (`BankTransaction.trust_ids`). Only when no id is shared do copies pair by `BankTransaction.pair_up`: the same amount, name (`name_key`), and posted time (`bank_transactions.posted_at`, SimpleFIN's `posted`) when both rows have one, plus the same purchase time (`transacted_at`) when both have that, then, for a row with no posted time, the same amount and name on the same day, then a day apart when that row and its copy are each other's only candidate. A row posted more than five days (`BankTransaction::PAIR_WINDOW`) after its purchase day never pairs by look. Identical rows pair one to one, so two coffees with two copies stay two rows. Old rows the replacement had no copy of stay in doubt (`BankTransaction.doubt_ids`) until a sync gives them a new id (`BankTransaction#adopt`), shows the bank kept theirs, or starts its report more than five days after their day, when no copy can arrive. A row that could be either of two copies stays as it is, so a doubtful case leaves a duplicate rather than deleting a purchase. A replacement row that copies an old row is folded into it (`BankTransaction#absorb`): the old row takes its id and stays visible unless both copies were deleted. When you deleted one copy, the one you kept brings its category, note, and Exclude, except that a row still paired as a transfer with a row you kept stays a Transfer. When both are visible, the old row keeps its own and fills blanks from the copy, and it counts unless both were excluded. A transfer pair on the copy moves to the old row when that row has no pair, and otherwise the partner loses its Transfer (`BankTransaction#unpair`). Rows stored before `posted_at` existed get it and `transacted_at` from the next sync that sends their id (`BankTransaction#note_times`). When no single account qualifies (the account was renamed, or two missing accounts share a name and bank), the row instead adds `It may now be one of the new accounts from this bank. Pick the same one.` and lists the pending accounts from the same bank in a `select` (`BankAccount#replacement_choices`), each option the name and balance (`Premier Savings · $1,234.56`, `replacement_choice_label`), beside `Same account`, which sends the chosen `replacement_id`. Those pending accounts stay visible as New rows. On a narrow screen the select and `Same account` wrap under `Stop syncing`, and the select truncates a long name. A report with no accounts marks nothing missing. A missing account with no `reported_at` offers no automatic replacement. Every SimpleFIN id an account had before is kept in `BankAccount::FormerId`, unique per SimpleFIN access like `bank_accounts.external_id`. Each account keeps SimpleFIN's `balance-date` (`bank_accounts.balance_date`). `Simplefin::Access#update_accounts` keeps one entry per account from a report (`BankAccount.freshest`, `BankAccount#outdated_in`): an entry under a former id wins only when its balance date is newer than the current id's entry, or, when the report leaves out the current id, newer than the account's stored balance date. A tie goes to the current id. The losing entries are dropped whole, name, balance, and transactions. A stale entry under a former id alone, as SimpleFIN sends while a sync is in progress, counts as not sending the account: it is not updated, and it is marked missing unless its last report (`reported_at`) was within a day, so one in-flight stale report shows no notice while a feed of only stale entries shows it after a day. A newer entry under a former id takes it back (`BankAccount#relink_back`): the account is not missing, no New row appears, and its new transactions arrive. Its trusted rows go in doubt and its rows still in doubt are trusted again (`BankTransaction.flip_doubt`), since those still carry the former id's transaction ids, so the same matching gives every row that id's transaction ids and a purchase sent under both ids is kept once. `test/models/bank_account/relinking_test.rb` covers it.
- Names: `BankTransaction::Name` turns SimpleFIN's `payee` and `description` into the transaction name (Zelle prefix and trailing reference codes dropped, the description used when the payee only says "Zelle Transfer") and the `bank_transactions.name_key` it stores for matching repeats. The demo's names are already clean, so its keys are `grocery store`, `johns fishin shack`, `you`.
- Rows: every `transactions/_transaction` row has a right slot `div.w-4`. Rows with a `BankTransaction` show the bank icon with `span.sr-only` "From your bank". Every kept row with no category and no transfer shows the left `div.bg-highlight` marker (`Transaction#needs_category?`, `Transaction.needing_category`), whether the bank added it, it was typed or imported, or a sync matched it to a typed row, and an excluded row still counts. Bank deposits wait with no category while typed or imported money in goes to `Income`. The bank review (`Simplefin::Review`) and `Rule#apply_to_uncategorized` still start from the rows the bank added, so typed, imported, and matched rows never show up in `Need a category` there and a rule never fills them.
- Category editing: every row's category (or "Uncategorized") is a `button[data-action="category-picker#open"]`. `category_picker_controller.js` sits on Recent's `ul` and on the ledger's rows wrapper with `data-category-picker-categories-value`, and puts one form in the clicked row's category line: `input[aria-label=Category][role=combobox]` and `#category-picker-list[role=listbox]` with `#category-picker-option-<n>` options filtered by prefix (`data-category-picker-categories-value` holds `[name, income?]` pairs), plus `New category <word>` (`New income category <word>` on money in). The categories matching the row's direction come first, then the rest under `Income` (on money out) or `Refund to` (on money in), see [transactions.md](transactions.md). Up and Down move, Tab completes, Enter or a click saves. An empty field highlights no option, so Enter saves it blank and clears the category; Down or Up then enter the list through `PATCH /transactions/:id/categorization` (`categorization[category]`, a name; blank clears; a new name creates the category), Escape closes and refocuses the button, blur closes without saving. The list ends with `Transfer` (hint `Not spending`, after a divider) whenever the typed text is a prefix of "transfer"; it posts `categorization[transfer]=1`. A transfer row reads `Transfer · <date>`, has its name and amount in `text-tertiary`, shows the ⇄ icon with `span.sr-only` "Transfer, not spending" in place of the bank icon, has no marker, and is left out of `Transaction.spending`, so the hero total and "Where it went" drop by its amount. A pick changes only that row. The redirect flashes `categorized_id` (like `added_id` after an add), and the page morphs, so "Where it went" and the marker update.
- Rules: after a pick on a row added by the bank (not a typed or matched one, and not a blank pick), the row that `categorized_id` names renders a strip under itself (`transactions/_rule_offer`, `[data-controller=dismiss]`): `Always put transactions named “<name>” in the <category chip> category?` or `Always mark transactions named “<name>” as <⇄ Transfer chip>?`, then `N more waiting now, plus future syncs.` (same-name rows on any of the user's bank accounts still needing a category) or `Applies to future syncs.`, `Just this one` (removes the strip in the browser; nothing is saved), and `Make a rule` (`POST /transactions/:id/rule`, `Transaction#make_rule`). Make a rule saves a `Rule` for the user and `name_key` with the row's categorization (`User#remember`), then `apply_to_uncategorized` fills every same-name row added by the bank on any of the user's bank accounts that still needs a category. It never overwrites a category or a transfer. No strip when the user already has a rule for the name with the same categorization (`Transaction#offers_rule?`). The picked row stays in its review list (and in the Overview block) for that one render, so the strip has a place, and on the review page the flash is kept until the loaded page frame that holds the row reloads. Moving on without answering saves nothing. Rows added on any account, including one linked later, take the rule's categorization first and fall back to the last category used for the name (`Rule::Book#categorization_for`, one rules query per sync, the book `Simplefin::Sync#rule_book` builds once). Proof: `mp.mjs query 'User.first.rules.map { [_1.name, _1.category&.name] }'` and `mp.mjs query 'User.first.transactions_named("grocery store").group(:category_id, :transfer).count'`. To watch a sync pick up a rule, delete one bank row and its transaction with `bin/rails runner`, then Sync now.
- Overview block: while any bank-added row from any sync still needs a category, any finished sync is unreviewed and added rows, or any possible duplicate is left (`Simplefin::Review#pending?`), the Overview shows `section#bank_review` above Recent (also while a sync runs, see Sync loader): the heading row has `From your bank`, a count pill with the number only when any rows need a category (`aria-label` and `title` `9 need a category`, `1 needs a category`; absent at zero), and `Show all` (`/simplefin/review`) on the right. Under it are up to 3 of those rows with an empty `Category` field in the category line, then the date and the bank account's full name (`field: true, account: true`). With none of them and an unreviewed sync that added rows (`Simplefin::Review#completable?`) it reads `Everything new has a category.`; with only possible duplicates left it doesn't. Last comes `N possible duplicates` with an arrow (`/simplefin/review?show=duplicates`) when there are any. The block has no `Done`; `Done` (`POST /simplefin/review/completion`) lives on the review page. A pick there runs the picker and the rule question like anywhere else. When the picked row is also in Recent, only the block's copy asks. The old sync toast and its `simplefin_sync_seen` cookie are gone.
- Transfer pairs: a new bank row that no rule gives a category pairs with an unpaired, unmatched, uncategorized bank row of the opposite amount in another of the user's accounts within 5 days (`BankTransaction.partner_for`, `PAIR_WINDOW`). Both halves become `Transfer`, so neither counts as spending or income, each row names the other account, and the Transfers review list shows them. A rule that marks the name a transfer still pairs; a rule that gives it a category does not (`Rule::Book#categorized?`). Giving one half a category dissolves the pair, and a paired row offers no rule. `test/models/bank_account/pairing_test.rb` covers it.
- Sync loader: while `Simplefin::Access#syncing?` (a running sync younger than `Simplefin::Sync::STALE_AFTER`), `section#bank_review` shows even with nothing to review, and its heading shows a spinner and `Syncing…` right after `From your bank` (and the count pill). With nothing pending the block has only the heading row, no `Everything new has a category.` The sync finishing refreshes the page and the loader goes away.
- Review: `GET /simplefin/review` (`Simplefin::ReviewsController#show`) lists the rows the bank added (`Simplefin::Access#review`, a `Simplefin::Review`). `Possible duplicates` holds both sides of the rows in the next bullet, `Need a category` holds rows from every sync that still have no category, so Done never hides them; `Categorized for you` and `Transfers` hold only rows from finished syncs with no `reviewed_at`. A trail `Overview` > `From your bank` (`nav[aria-label=Breadcrumb]`, `Overview` links to `/`) sits above the title row, which has `From your bank`, then `Synced N min ago` and `Done` while unreviewed syncs added rows (`Simplefin::Review#completable?`). `Done` (`POST /simplefin/review/completion`) stamps `reviewed_at` on those syncs and redirects to the Overview, where the block stays until every row has a category and no possible duplicate is left. `nav[aria-label=Lists]` copies the Period nav: `Possible duplicates N`, `Need a category N`, `Categorized for you N`, `Transfers N`, picked with `?show=duplicates|needing_category|categorized|transfers`; anything else opens the first list with rows, and the current one carries `aria-current=true`. Rows of the need-a-category list render with `field: true, account: true` (an empty `Category` field, then `· <date> · <bank account full name>`). `BankAccount#full_name` puts the institution before the account name unless the name already shares a word with it (`Chase CREDIT CARD`, but `SimpleFIN Savings`); every account label uses it. Below `sm` the account drops to its own line under the field. A list of transactions shows 50 rows per page through `geared_pagination`: `turbo-frame#<list>_page_1` holds them, and its last child is a lazy `turbo-frame#<list>_page_2` (`loading=lazy`, `refresh=morph`) that loads `?show=<list>&page=2` when it scrolls into view, and so on. A pick reloads the loaded page frames in place after the morph, so the scroll position stays. From `@2xl` of `main`'s width (672px, the Help drawer narrows it) the Rules column sits beside the list (sticky): `Rules` with its count, `Bank transactions with these names get that category.`, then one row per `Rule`, newest first: `“<name>”`, an arrow, the category chip (or an outlined `⇄ Transfer` chip), `N rows`, plus `· just now` and the soft highlight when the rule changed in the last minute, and `Forget` (`DELETE /rules/:id`, `RulesController#destroy`), which deletes the rule and leaves the rows as they are. With no rules it reads `Rules you make show up here.` With nothing unreviewed, nothing needing a category, and no possible duplicates it reads `Nothing new from your bank. Back to Overview`.
- Possible duplicates: the first review list (`Transaction::Duplicates`, read by `User#possible_duplicates` and `Simplefin::Review#duplicates`), derived on every read, so it covers import then sync, sync then import, and rows already there. An entry (`Transaction::Duplicate`) is one typed or imported row and the bank row or rows it may repeat: one row of the same amount at most 7 days apart, else two that add up, else three or more equal rows with one name that add up, where each is at most 3 days from the typed row and 1 day from the others. Both sides have one currency and direction, count in the totals, and are kept; the bank side was added by the bank (not matched). A row sits in one entry at a time: fewest bank rows first, then the closest, then the lower ids. Under `Same transaction keeps the bank's rows and removes the one you added.` each `li#duplicate_transaction_<id>` has the typed row with `<date> · From <filename>` or `<date> · Typed in`, the bank rows with `<date> · <account>` and the bank icon, `Adds up to $65.57` when there are several, `Keep both` (`POST /transactions/:id/separation`) and `Same transaction` (`POST /transactions/:id/merge`), whose `aria-label` adds the row's name, date and amount. The list shows up to 50 and the tab counts them all. Empty, it reads `No possible duplicates.` `Same transaction` discards the typed row, gives each bank row its category and note where the bank row has none, and uses the bank rows up. `Keep both` never offers those rows together again; a later bank row is offered. Each answer is a `Transaction::DuplicateAnswer` per bank row, which goes away with either row (Undo of the import included). An answer acts only while its rows are still offered together (`User#merge_duplicate`, `User#keep_both`), so a second click or a stale page changes nothing. `test/models/transaction/duplicates_test.rb` and `test/integration/possible_duplicates_test.rb` cover it.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Sidebar, Settings, Bank sync, Connect your bank. Open SimpleFIN, copy a setup token, paste it. Pick accounts, choose Today or 90 days ago, Start syncing. Settings flips from `Syncing…` to `Synced just now` by itself when the job finishes.

## Driving it with mp.mjs

- A demo token: `curl -s https://beta-bridge.simplefin.org/info/developers | grep -oE 'aHR0[A-Za-z0-9+/=]{60,}' | sort -u`. Each token claims once. A reload of that page gives a new one.
- `mp.mjs goto /settings --port N`, `mp.mjs click 'turbo-frame#bank_sync a' --port N`, `mp.mjs wait 'turbo-frame#bank_sync' --text 'Paste the token' --port N`.
- `mp.mjs paste '#simplefin_access_setup_token' "$TOKEN" --port N`, `mp.mjs click 'turbo-frame#bank_sync button.bg-action' --port N`, then `mp.mjs wait 'turbo-frame#bank_sync' --text 'Pick the accounts' --timeout 20000 --port N`. Paste the same token again and click Connect for the rejected state: `--text 'already used'`. `paste` adds to what is in the field, so after the bad paste clear it (see `paste` in SKILL.md) before pasting the real token.
- Choose 90 days: `mp.mjs click 'label:has(input[value="90_days"])' --port N`. Start: `mp.mjs click 'turbo-frame#bank_sync button.bg-action' --port N`, then `mp.mjs wait 'turbo-frame#bank_sync' --text 'Synced just now' --timeout 30000 --port N`.
- Account rows: `Bring in 90 days` is `turbo-frame#bank_sync form[action="/bank_accounts/<id>/history"] button`. On a New row, Skip and Sync it are two forms with the same action `/bank_accounts/<id>`, so pick by the hidden status: `form:has(input[name="bank_account[status]"][value=synced]) button` is Sync it and `[value=skipped]` is Skip. The demo never leaves an account New; for a drive, set one with `bin/rails runner 'BankAccount.find(ID).update_columns(status: "pending", starts_on: nil)'`.
- Missing rows: the demo never changes an id. For a drive, run `bin/rails runner 'BankAccount.find(ID).update_columns(missing_since: Time.current, reported_at: 1.day.ago)'`, and then create a second account with the same name and institution and status `pending` to get `Same account`. Its button is `form[action="/bank_accounts/<id>/replacement"] button`; `Stop syncing` is in `form[action="/bank_accounts/<id>"]`.
- Possible duplicates: the sync matches a typed row dated 5 days before to 1 day after the bank row by itself, so add the typed row after the sync. Pick a bank row with `mp.mjs query 'Transaction.needing_category.first.then { [_1.name, _1.amount_in_cents, _1.occurred_on] }'`, add its amount and date under another name in the composer, and open `/simplefin/review`. The buttons are `li#duplicate_transaction_<id> form[action$=merge] button` and `form[action$=separation] button`. Proof: `mp.mjs query '[User.first.possible_duplicates.size, Transaction::DuplicateAnswer.pluck(:typed_transaction_id, :bank_transaction_id, :same)]'`.
- A sync of the demo finishes before the next page paints, so `Syncing…` is rarely seen. To look at it, create an unfinished sync with `bin/rails runner 'puts Simplefin::Access.first.syncs.create!.id'`, `goto /` (the spinner beside `From your bank`) or `/settings` (a disabled `Syncing…` button and no `/simplefin/sync` form), then delete that sync by id.
- Proof of a sync that added rows: `mp.mjs query 's = Simplefin::Sync.last; [s.failure, s.bank_transactions.count(&:unmatched?), s.bank_transactions.count(&:matched?)]'` and `mp.mjs query '[Transaction.count, BankTransaction.count]'`. The demo gives about 170 transactions per account for 90 days.
- Sync now: `mp.mjs click 'turbo-frame#bank_sync form[action="/simplefin/sync"] button' --port N` (the page goes to `/`), poll `Simplefin::Sync.finished.count` until it grows, and compare the counts. A second sync of the same data adds 0 rows.
- Disconnect: `mp.mjs click 'turbo-frame#bank_sync form[action="/simplefin/access"] button' --port N`. Transactions and bank transactions stay.

## Gotchas

- Check `mp.mjs query 'Simplefin::Access.first&.status'` before a drive. When the dev database already holds an `active` access, it may be the developer's real bank, so do not Disconnect it or claim a new token over it. Without touching it you can still drive the steady-state section, a bad paste (`paste ... 'hello'` reads `That isn't a SimpleFIN setup token.` and saves nothing), the review page, and a pick on a bank row with its rule strip (`Just this one`, then clear the pick).
- Disconnect during a running sync abandons it, and the bank's late answer is dropped (`Simplefin::Sync#run_now`).
- In the picker, Cancel is also a submit button in the frame and disconnects. Target `button.bg-action` for Start syncing, never `button[type=submit]`.
- The demo has no 402, 403, or errlist states. To look at the lapsed notice, run `Simplefin::Sync.last.update_column(:failure, "lapsed")` with `bin/rails runner`, take the shot, and set it back to `nil`. To look at `revoked`, set `Simplefin::Access.sole.update_column(:status, "revoked")` and back to `active`. Do not clear `access_url`, or you need a new token.
- The demo dates some transactions a day or two in the future, so the newest bank rows can sit after today.
- Development runs jobs with the in-process async adapter, so a sync runs inside the server a moment after the request. The recurring hourly sweep (`Simplefin::Access.sync_due`) is only scheduled in production.
- Jobs read the time zone from `users.time_zone`. `time_zone_controller.js` sends `PATCH /time_zone` when the browser's zone differs from the saved one; requests themselves only read the `time_zone` cookie.
- After Done the block and review page stay while any bank row needs a category or any possible duplicate is left. To bring back the Categorized and Transfers lists after Done, run `Simplefin::Sync.last.update_columns(reviewed_at: nil)` with `bin/rails runner`. The demo resends the same rows, so a fresh Sync now brings nothing new; delete a bank row and its transaction with `bin/rails runner` first to make it bring one.
