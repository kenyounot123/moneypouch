# Feature map

- [sign-in.md](sign-in.md). The sign-in form, wrong-password alert, sign out.
- [overview.md](overview.md). The signed-in Overview with the composer and sidebar.
- [onboarding.md](onboarding.md). Create account, the first transaction page, Skip for now, landing on the Overview.
- [sidebar.md](sidebar.md). The collapsible sidebar, its cookie, tooltips, and the palette re-centering.
- [theme.md](theme.md). Light and dark switch on the Settings page.
- [add-transaction.md](add-transaction.md). The composer and demo data.
- [transactions.md](transactions.md). The month ledger at `/transactions`: totals, month switcher, filters, day-grouped rows, and the phone sheet.
- [voucher.md](voucher.md). How typed shorthand becomes a name, amount, date, and category.
- [composer.md](composer.md). The ⌘K palette, preview, add and undo, Tab completion, `#` list, `@` calendar, with their handles, lane recipes, and perf budgets.
- [bank-sync.md](bank-sync.md). Connect SimpleFIN in Settings, pick accounts, sync, Disconnect, the bank icon and marker on rows, and the sync toast.
- [import.md](import.md). Import a CSV or xlsx file: the Import page and its drop zone, the Columns grid, the Categories step with its tally, Import, the latest import's Undo, and the empty Overview's ways in.
