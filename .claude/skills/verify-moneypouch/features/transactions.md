# Transactions ledger

## Sub-features

- `/transactions` shows one month, `?month=YYYY-MM`. A missing or unreadable month is this month, and a future month clamps to this month (`Month` in `app/models/month.rb`).
- Header: `Spent in <Month>` over the total in `.text-display`, then `dl dd` Income, Net, and Transactions when the main column is at least `@xl` (576px), or one `Income … · Net … · N transactions` line below that. All three follow the filters. `spent` matches the Overview's month total, income is `Transaction.incoming` (see [overview.md](overview.md)), and `net` is every counted row (not a transfer, not excluded), so `net` is `income − spent`. Net carries its sign (`−$492.43`, `+$…` in `text-success`); Income does not. Money-in amounts on rows read `+$…` in `text-success`, transfers and excluded rows tertiary, and a positive day total is green.
- Month switcher `nav[aria-label=Month]`: `‹ Sep`, the current `October 2026` (`[aria-current]`), and `Nov ›` only for a past month. Phones get a compact copy in its own row under the top bar: bare `‹` and `›` (named by `aria-label`) around `Oct 2026`. Month links keep the filters.
- Filters are a GET form: `#query` (name or note, case-insensitive), `select#account` (each bank account, `typed` for rows with no bank row), `select#category` (each category by id, `none` for uncategorized), each select submitting on change. The chip `a[aria-pressed]` (`Needs a category · N` from `@xl`, `N uncategorized` below) toggles `needs=1` and counts the filtered rows that need a category, which are exactly the rows with the gold marker. It is absent when that count is 0 and the filter is off. Below `@xl` the selects hide behind the `Filters` label (`label[for=filters_toggle]`).
- Rows group by day, newest first. From `@xl` each day is a `section` with a 96px date gutter (day total only when the day has more than one row) and `li#transaction_<id>` rows 40px tall with lanes for the marker, the name and note, the category, the account (from `@4xl`, 896px), and the amount. A row that needs a category shows the bordered `Category` field, any other row its dotted category. Both open `category-picker` (see [bank-sync.md](bank-sync.md) for its keys). Its list puts the categories that match the row's direction first, then the rest under a heading, `Income` on money out and `Refund to` on money in, then `Transfer` (`Not spending`). A new name reads `New category <word>`, or `New income category <word>` on money in, which saves an income category. Enter on an empty field saves it blank and the row reads `Uncategorized`. Hover or focus shows `Add a note` (when there is no note) and a `Delete` `button_to` that asks for confirmation. Below `@xl` rows are two lines under a full-width day header, with no hover controls.
- Excluded: from `@xl`, hover or focus shows the `exclude` icon and `Exclude from totals` (the `include` icon and `Include in totals` on an excluded row) beside `Delete`; below `@3xl` the text hides and the icon carries it as `aria-label` and `title`. It is a `button_to` to `PATCH /transactions/:id/exclusion` (`transaction[excluded]=true|false`, `Transactions::ExclusionsController`). An excluded row dims its name and amount, shows an `Excluded` tag after the name (hidden while hovered), and drops out of the header totals. Below `@xl` the line reads `Category · Excluded` and the phone sheet has `Exclude from totals` or `Include in totals` next to `Delete`, with `Excluded` in its date line. The day total (`@day_totals`) still sums every row.
- Pages: a busy month renders whole days, newest first, until it holds at least 100 rows (`Transaction::Page`). The rest follows in nested `turbo-frame#days_<YYYY-MM-DD>[loading=lazy][refresh=morph]` frames, `?before=<date>` meaning the days before that date, each loaded as it scrolls into view. Header totals and the count cover the whole month. A refresh keeps loaded frames and reloads them; one whose start date is no longer a page boundary comes back empty, and the new boundary's frame loads in its place.
- The list ends at its last day with no footer. Empty month: `Nothing in <Month>.` No match: `No transactions match.`
- Back to top: `button[aria-label='Back to top']` (`back_to_top_controller.js`) is sticky at the right edge, 12px above the composer below `md` and centered on the composer's row from `lg`. It sits in the flow before the composer, so at the end of the list it never covers a row. It fades in (`[data-shown]`) once the window has scrolled more than one viewport and scrolls smoothly to the top (instantly with reduced motion). Short months never show it.
- Phone sheet: below `@xl` each row's line is covered by `a[data-turbo-frame=sheet]` to `/transactions/:id/edit`, which renders `turbo-frame#sheet` into `dialog[data-controller=sheet]`. `sheet_controller.js` opens it on `turbo:frame-load` and closes it on a backdrop click, Escape, any form submit inside it, and the morph that follows a save. The sheet holds the category picker at `data-category-picker-size-value=large`, `form#sheet_note`, Delete, and `button[form=sheet_note]` Done, which saves the note.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Sign in and click Transactions in the sidebar, or the count link under Recent. On a phone, the page opens from that count link.

## Driving it with mp.mjs

- Desktop rows hide their phone line, so target the visible lane: `$M hover '#transaction_N div.h-10 p.font-mono'`, then `$M click '#transaction_N div.h-10 button[aria-label=Delete]'` for Delete, `'#transaction_N div.h-10 form[action$="/exclusion"] button'` for the flag, or `'#transaction_N div.h-10 button[data-action="category-picker#open"]'` for the picker.
- Selects: `mp.mjs` has no select command. Set the value and dispatch `change` with `js` to exercise the auto-submit.
- Sheet: `$M resize 390 844`, `$M click '#transaction_N a[data-turbo-frame=sheet]'`, `$M wait 'turbo-frame#sheet h2' --text ''`, then read `document.querySelector("dialog[data-controller=sheet]").open`.
- Read the picker's list with `$M js '[...document.querySelectorAll("#category-picker-list > *")].map(e => e.textContent.trim())'`.
- The `Excluded` tag hides while its row is hovered, so `wait '#transaction_N' --text Excluded` times out with the pointer still on the row. Read `$M query 'Transaction.find(N).excluded'` or move the pointer away first.

## Gotchas

- Other screens refresh at commit: `Transaction` broadcasts `refresh` on `[user, :transactions]` from `after_commit`, not through `broadcasts_refreshes_to`'s debounced job, and `application.js` sets Turbo's refresh debounce to 50 ms. Bulk writes (sync, import) wrap in `Transaction.broadcasting_once_for`.
- A month needs more than 100 rows to page. `bin/rails "dev:transactions[12000]"` gives about 1,000 per month.
- Rows added by the bank have no shorthand, so deleting one leaves nothing in the composer to undo with. Restore with `Transaction#restore` from `bin/rails runner`.
- The container breakpoints are on `main`, so a collapsed sidebar can switch the layout at the same window width.
